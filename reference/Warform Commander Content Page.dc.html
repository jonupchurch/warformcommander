ABOUT

Built for the plan, not the wallet.

Warform Commander is a sci-fi tactics wargame where every battle is decided before the first shot — by composition, counters, loadouts, and positioning.

WARFORM STUDIO·UPDATED JUL 2026·4 MIN READ

We started Warform Commander with one stubborn belief: that a strategy game can be deep, competitive, and fair — without ever asking you to pay for power. The genre we love too often slides into pay-to-win. We're designing against that from the ground up, not patching it in later.

Why we're making this

The best moments in tactics games happen in your head — the read on your opponent, the counter you saw coming, the loadout that flips a matchup. We wanted to build a game that lives entirely in that space: assemble five machines, kit and position them, dial in their behavior, then watch your plan execute. Skill is planning. Reflexes never enter the room.

"A sharp newcomer should be able to beat a sloppy veteran. Between equal players, dedication breaks the tie — never the wallet."

What we believe

01

Non-P2W by construction. Power is capped and lateral. Money buys looks and convenience — the best gear is earned only.

02

Depth from configuration. A small roster, enormous variety — variants, equipment, behavior dials, and positioning do the work.

03

Fairness is verified, not hoped. Because battles are simulatable, a Monte-Carlo balancer proves matchups are fair before players find the cracks.

04

Content comes from players. Every defense you face is another commander's plan — the async ladder is never empty.

Where we're headed

The first build focuses on the core: a real-time tick simulation, the full army toolset unlocked, and an async PvP ladder with a practice sandbox. Everything else — PvE, progression, cosmetics, commanders — comes after the fundamentals feel right. We'd rather ship a small game that's fair than a big one that isn't.

Want to help shape it? Join the community and get in on the closed ladder test.
